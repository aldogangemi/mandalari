Automated Analysis of 20th Century Italian Asylum Patient Writings

Methods and Results from the PsyCreat Study

1. A corpus of patient-authored texts.

2. Automated extraction of conceptual frames based on an ontology.

3. Automated scoring of text creativity and authoriality.

4. Clustering of frames, texts, and authors and their relation to scores.

1. The Corpus: Patient-Authored Texts

The study is based on a unique collection of texts authored by patients at an Italian asylum during the 20th century (from concatenated_file.txt).

Example Text:

ID:1
Author: Michele Pappalardo
Title: Untitled
Date: 8 agosto 1929
Type: Letter

Carissimo Calogero
Maisano sta benissimo a efa sua, arzente a Fioravanti e Orlandino del Leone.
Ozia ala ordita da Sale vafio ornante pedi zeili di chi ofelio il Monte 
Sion al Vezzo antico a zaino fodero uvile mazzo fedele Vacircato a Vaisa 
opera demoni a efe.
...

This text (doc_1) is described in allcreativitydata.tsv as a "dense, systematic, and highly elaborated private language (cryptolect)."

2. The Method: The Ontology Schema

We first built an ontology (psycreat.ttl) to formally model the relationships between authors, texts, and concepts.

Key Ontology Classes & Properties:


:Document rdf:type owl:Class ;
    rdfs:label "Document" .

:Author rdf:type owl:Class ;
    rdfs:label "Author" .

:Frame rdf:type owl:Class ;
    rdfs:label "Frame" .

:isAuthorOf rdf:type owl:ObjectProperty ;
    rdfs:domain :Author ;
    rdfs:range :Document .

:denotes rdf:type owl:ObjectProperty ;
    rdfs:domain :Document ;
    rdfs:range :Frame .

:hasMainDiseaseCondition rdf:type owl:ObjectProperty ;
    rdfs:domain :Author .

2. The Method: Automated Frame Extraction

Using this ontology, we processed the corpus to create a Knowledge Graph (psycreatkg.ttl) by extracting specific conceptual frames from the texts.

Example Extracted Frame (doc_371):


kg:antireligioussentiment_4 rdf:type :Anti_religious_Sentiment ;
    rdfs:label "antireligioussentiment_4" ;
    :evokesFrame :Anti_religious_Sentiment ;
    :appearsInDocument kg:doc_371 ;
    :hasProtagonist kg:Nioi_Antonio ;
    :hasTarget kg:Paolo_VI_detto_santo_padre ;
    :hasTarget kg:Giudeo_Cristo .

kg:grandiosedelusionsreincarnationhistorical_2 rdf:type :Grandiose_Delusions ;
    :appearsInDocument kg:doc_371 ;
    :hasTheme "un secondo Caio Giulio Cesare moderno!" .

3. The Method: Scoring Creativity & Authoriality

Each text was quantitatively scored for "Creativity" (divergence from convention) and "Authoriality" (rhetorical control) using data from allcreativitydata.tsv.

Key Metrics & Example Scores:

Metric Description Doc_1 (Pappalardo) Doc_371 (Nioi)
Creativity_minmax_z Divergence from convention 0.999 (Highest) 0.350
Authoriality_WAAS_minmax Authorial / rhetorical control 0.811 (High) 0.710 (High)
CreatWaas_minmax Combined Creativity/Authoriality 0.998 (Highest) 0.801 (High)

4. The Analysis: Conceptual Clusters

Clustering frames, texts, and authors (from mandalariclusters.md) revealed two dominant, opposing authorial profiles.

Cluster A: The "Visionary"

Conflict & Persecution (Literal Belief)

Frames: :Persecution, :Political_Rebellion_Threat, :Grandiose_Delusions, :Conspiracy

Scores: Tends toward High Creativity (high divergence) but Low Authoriality (low metaphorical control).

Cluster B: The "Classic Artist"

Art & Metaphor (Rhetorical Control)

Frames: :Poetry, :Fable, :Political_Satire, :Fantastical_Mythological_Journey

Scores: Tends toward Low Creativity (conventional forms) but High Authoriality (high rhetorical control).

Finding 1: The "Visionary" Profile

Author: Pappalardo Michele

Pappalardo is the archetype of the "Visionary." His text (doc_1) is a systematic, private language ("cryptolect") that is taken as literal truth.

Dominant Frames

  • :Cryptolect (Private Language)
  • :Persecutory_Delusion
  • :Conspiracy

Quantitative Profile

  • Creativity Score: 0.999 (Rank #1)
  • Authoriality Score: 0.811 (Rank #3)
  • Profile: Maximum divergence, presented with literal belief.

Finding 2: The "Classic Artist" Profile

Author: Sacco Giuseppe

Sacco is the archetype of the "Classic Artist." He uses conventional literary forms (fables, poems) with clear rhetorical control and metaphorical distance.

Dominant Frames

  • :Fable
  • :Poetry
  • :Novella

Quantitative Profile

  • Creativity Score: 0.288 (Low)
  • Authoriality Score: 0.865 (Rank #1)
  • Profile: High rhetorical skill and control over metaphors.

Finding 3: The "Hybrid" Author

Author: Sacca Antonino

Sacca Antonino is a rare "hybrid" who scores at the top of *both* lists, demonstrating mastery of both conceptual clusters.

"Classic Artist" Frames

  • :Political_Satire
  • :Fantastical_Mythological_Journey
  • (High metaphorical control)

"Visionary" Frames

  • :Anti_fascism
  • :Political_Rebellion_Threat
  • :Persecution ("luogo di tortura")
  • (100% literal belief)

Scores: CreatWaas_minmax: 0.804 (Rank #2), Authoriality_WAAS_minmax: 0.831 (Rank #2)

Conclusions & Implications

✓ Automated methods can successfully model, extract, and score complex conceptual frames from psychiatric texts.

✓ "Creativity" (divergence) and "Authoriality" (control) are distinct, measurable constructs that are not mutually exclusive.

✓ Conceptual frame clusters (e.g., "Visionary" vs. "Artist") correlate strongly with these quantitative scores, linking semantics to metrics.

✓ This provides a new, scalable method for digital humanities and the computational study of "outsider art" and unique textual production.
